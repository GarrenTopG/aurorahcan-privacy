Privacy Policy & Data Rights

Effective Date: March 2025

Organisation: Aurorah Community Action Network (Aurorah CAN NPC)

Application: Aurorah CAN Digital Hub (Android)

Aurorah CAN NPC is committed to protecting your personal information in compliance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and global data protection standards including the Google Play Developer Policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with the Aurorah CAN Digital Hub app, we collect personal information that you directly provide to us, including:

  • Account & Profile Information: Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Account Password.
  • Community Assistance & Need Requests: Geographic Location / Area, Phone Contact, Urgency Level, and specific details of support requested.
  • Volunteer Applications: Skills, availability, area of residence, and contact preferences.
  • Donation Records: Donation pledge categories, pledged amounts, payment method selected, and transaction references. (Note: Sensitive card payment tokens are processed securely via encrypted third-party payment gateways; we do not store full credit card numbers on our servers).

2. How Your Data is Used

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate non-profit community coordination purposes:

  • To verify user accounts and manage user profiles.
  • To review, coordinate, and fulfill community assistance requests in Kuils River and surrounding areas.
  • To match volunteers with local community outreaches.
  • To record, verify, and issue receipts for community donations.
  • To send critical push notifications or updates regarding your requests or applications.

3. Data Protection and Encryption

We prioritize security:

  • In Transit: All communications between the app and our servers are encrypted using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • On Device: Local session tokens and sensitive credentials on your device are protected using Android’s EncryptedSharedPreferences backed by AES-256 hardware keys.
  • No Selling of Data: Your personal data is never sold, rented, or traded to third parties or commercial entities.

4. Account & Data Deletion Rights

Under POPIA and Google Play policies, you have full control over your data. You may request the deletion of your account and all associated personal records at any time through either of the following methods:

Option A: In-App Account Deletion (Instant)

  1. Open the Aurorah CAN Digital Hub Android app.
  2. Navigate to the Profile / Settings screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account & Personal Data".
  4. Confirm deletion in the popup dialog. Your account and personal details will be immediately removed.

Option B: Web-Based Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the app, submit an email deletion request to our Data Protection Officer:

Email: info@aurorahcan.org

Subject Line: Account and Data Deletion Request

Please include your registered full name and email address. Requests submitted via email are processed within 7 business days.

5. What Data is Erased Upon Deletion

When an account deletion request is processed:

  • Your account profile, name, email, phone number, and password hashes are permanently deleted from our active user database.
  • Active volunteer applications and assistance requests tied to your User ID are unlinked or removed.
  • Anonymized financial transaction totals may be retained in aggregated form solely for statutory tax and non-profit audit compliance as required by South African law.
