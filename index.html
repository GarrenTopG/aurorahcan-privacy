Welcome to our privacy policy

Aurorah Community Action Network (Aurorah CAN NPC) respects your privacy and is committed to responsible handling of your personal information. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Aurorah CAN Digital Hub Android application.

01

Information We Collect

The information you share with us.

When you interact with the Aurorah CAN Digital Hub, we may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when registering an account, requesting assistance, volunteering, or making a donation.

  • Account and profile information: Your full name, email address, phone number, and account authentication information.
  • Community assistance requests: Your geographic area, contact details, urgency level, and information about the support you require.
  • Volunteer applications: Your skills, availability, area of residence, and contact preferences.
  • Donation records: Donation categories, pledged amounts, selected payment methods, and transaction references. Full card details are not stored on our servers as described in our payment-processing practices.
i
Information you provide

Please provide accurate information and avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal details in assistance requests or other submissions.

02

How Your Data Is Used

Using information to support our community.

We use your personal information for legitimate community coordination and organisational purposes, including:

  • Creating, verifying, and managing user accounts.
  • Reviewing and coordinating community assistance requests in Kuils River and surrounding areas.
  • Matching volunteers with relevant community initiatives and outreach activities.
  • Recording and verifying donations and issuing receipts where applicable.
  • Communicating important updates about your requests, applications, or account.
  • Maintaining platform security, preventing misuse, and meeting applicable legal obligations.

We aim to collect and use only the information reasonably necessary for these purposes. Where applicable, processing is based on a lawful justification under South African data protection legislation.

03

Data Protection and Security

Safeguarding information against unauthorised access.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

  • Secure communications: Communications between the application and our servers should use HTTPS/TLS encryption where supported by the deployed service.
  • Device security: Authentication tokens and sensitive information stored on your device should be protected using appropriate Android security mechanisms.
  • Payment security: Where third-party payment gateways are used, payment information is handled according to the gateway's applicable security practices.
  • Responsible data sharing: We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties for their own commercial marketing purposes.
!
Security has its limits

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We work to reduce risks and respond appropriately to security incidents.

04

Account and Data Deletion

Your right to request the removal of your information.

Your data. Your choice.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information, subject to applicable legal retention requirements. Use either of the methods below.

Option A

In-App Account Deletion

If the feature is available in your installed version of the application, follow these steps:

  1. Open the Aurorah CAN Digital Hub Android application.
  2. Navigate to your Profile or Settings screen.
  3. Select Delete Account & Personal Data.
  4. Review the consequences and confirm your deletion request.

Your request will be handled according to the application's deletion process and applicable retention requirements.

Option B

Email Deletion Request

If you cannot access the application, contact our organisation by email.

Include your registered full name and email address so that we can locate your account and verify your request.

Email subject Account and Data Deletion Request
✉ Request Data Deletion

Processing time: Requests are targeted for processing within 7 business days, subject to verification and applicable requirements.

i
What happens next?

We may need to verify your identity before processing a request. We will communicate any relevant limitations where information must be retained by law.

05

What Happens to Your Data?

Deletion, retention, and legal obligations.

Once a verified account deletion request has been processed, we will delete or de-identify personal information associated with your account where appropriate and technically feasible.

  • Account information: Your profile details and authentication information will be removed from active account records, subject to applicable retention requirements.
  • Volunteer applications and assistance requests: Records linked to your account will be deleted, anonymised, or otherwise handled appropriately, subject to operational and legal obligations.
  • Financial and audit records: Certain donation, accounting, or transaction records may need to be retained to comply with legal, tax, financial, or audit requirements.
  • Backups and security records: Information held in protected backups or security logs may remain for a limited period where necessary, before being deleted or overwritten according to the applicable retention process.
i
Important information

Deleting an account does not necessarily mean every record must be erased immediately. Any retained information should be limited to what is legally required or otherwise lawfully justified and protected against unnecessary access or use.

Questions about your privacy?

If you have questions about this policy, need help with a deletion request, or wish to exercise your personal information rights, please contact Aurorah CAN.
